# Standards Basis for Test Case Design

Research note for maintaining this skill, not a per-feature checklist. Sources were checked on 2026-10-09. Use the project's approved versions and contractual/regulatory requirements where they differ. These are methodology sources, not product specifications oracles.

| Source | Relevant guidance | How this skill applies it | Limit |

| [ISTQB CTFL v4.0.1 syllabus](https://istqb.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/ISTQB_CTFL_Syllabus_v4.0.1.pdf) and the [official glossary](https://glossary.istqb.org/en_US/home) | Shared testing vocabulary, test design techniques, and the distinction between a test basis, test conditions, cases, and coverage. | Derive test conditions from approved sources; select techniques to fit the model; trace conditions and risks to cases. ## Applying the sources

1. Treat the approved product specification, API/design contracts, and security/accessibility requirements as the behavior oracle. Standards and checklists help discover conditions and choose techniques; they do not authorize inventing an expected status, message, threshold, or policy.
2. Keep each case reproducible and independently verifiable: preconditions, data, steps/actions, expected result, and postcondition when state changes. Put shared teardown in suite setup when it applies to multiple cases.
3. Define the set of requirements, test conditions, transitions, or risks being counted before reporting coverage. State method, strength, exclusions, and remaining risks; avoid claims of exhaustive coverage.
4. Tailor the documentation to the actual feature and project convention. Do not copy every standard/checklist item into every suite, and do not claim formal standards compliance without a separate compliance assessment.
